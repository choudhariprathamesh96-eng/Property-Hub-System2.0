Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how Property Hub collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Introduction

Property Hub is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number and profile details.
  • Property Information: Property listings, descriptions, prices, images and locations.
  • Usage Information: Search history, viewed properties and website activity.
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system and IP address for security purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your Property Hub account.
  • To connect buyers, renters and property owners.
  • To improve our website and user experience.
  • To respond to your enquiries and support requests.
  • To send important account updates and notifications.
  • To prevent fraud and improve website security.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your personal information. Although no online system is completely secure, Property Hub continuously works to keep your data safe from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

Cookies Policy

Property Hub uses cookies to remember your preferences, improve website performance and provide a better browsing experience. You can disable cookies through your browser settings if you prefer.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services such as maps, analytics and payment gateways. These providers have their own privacy policies governing the information they collect.

Your Rights

  • Access and update your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account (subject to applicable laws).
  • Opt out of promotional emails and notifications.
  • Request information about how your data is used.
